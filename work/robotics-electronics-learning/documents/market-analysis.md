## Proposed JTBD and value proposition
Learn to build electronic and robotic systems without an electronics degree or expensive laboratory. Gain confidence and a practical portfolio. Before: fragmented learning, dependence on examples, debugging friction. How: progressive curriculum, interactive labs, homework and assessment. After: independent engineering work. Alternatives: videos, courses, kits, standalone simulators and formal education.
