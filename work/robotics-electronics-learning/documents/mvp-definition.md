# MVP definition — exploratory, not approved

Prove educational outcomes before broad simulation coverage. Earlier discussion proposed 20–30 lessons and three progressive projects; an earlier ESP32-only concept suggested 30 lessons/homework, five capstones, three environments and 15–20 components. These are alternative exploratory targets, not estimates or commitments.

Proposed first slice: interactive concepts, guided practice, independent assignment and feedback; select concrete first learner/project after discovery. Potential non-goals: full simulator replacement, advanced ROS2/Isaac Sim integration in first release.

Success measures to validate: independent task completion, learning gains, retention and willingness to commit time/money.
