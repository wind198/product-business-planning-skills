# Idea → opportunity → MVP

Use pinned skills: `idea-generation`, `problem-validation`, `market-segments`, `value-proposition`, `feature-prioritization`, `prd`.

Start with business pain (if available), target customer clues, existing code/products and constraints. Research and propose several opportunity and segment candidates; prioritize the riskiest assumption. Design cheap experiments and measure evidence before overbuilding. Define minimal user journey, non-goals, success metrics, feature shortlist and testable acceptance. Produce `idea-brief.md`, `mvp-definition.md` and, when useful, `srs.md`. Allow provisional drafts with visibly unresolved risks.
